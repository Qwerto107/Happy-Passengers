Happy Passengers 0.8.0

Speeding and both G-force tiers apply no passenger satisfaction penalty.
超速和两档 G 力均不扣除乘客满意度。

The penalty-free driving reward distance is reduced from 30 km to 10 km.
连续无扣分行驶的奖励间隔从 30 km 缩短至 10 km。

Each qualifying reward retains the original increase of 6–12 percentage points.
每次符合条件的奖励仍按原版增加 6–12 个百分点。

Reward eligibility, distance tracking and satisfaction limits follow the original game logic.
奖励触发资格、里程累计及满意度上限沿用原版游戏逻辑。

All five chassis variants across the three official coaches receive lower centers of gravity.
三款官方客车的五种底盘均降低重心。

MAN Lion's Coach: 1.25 to 0.95 on both chassis variants.
MAN Lion's Coach：两种底盘均从 1.25 调整为 0.95。

Scania Touring: 1.20 to 0.90 on both chassis variants.
Scania Touring：两种底盘均从 1.20 调整为 0.90。

Volvo 9700 DD: 1.40 to 1.05.
Volvo 9700 DD：从 1.40 调整为 1.05。

Lower centers of gravity aim to improve cornering stability, but do not guarantee protection from rollovers.
降低重心旨在改善过弯稳定性，但不能保证车辆不会侧翻。

Chassis weight, suspension, tires, steering, power and passenger capacity retain their original values.
底盘重量、悬挂、轮胎、转向、动力和载客量保持原值。

Original passenger and other game sounds are retained, with no custom praise messages or voices.
保留原版乘客及其他游戏声音，不包含自定义称赞提示或语音。

Collisions, wear, lateness, traffic offences and extreme-impact job-failure rules remain unchanged.
碰撞、磨损、晚点、交通违章和严重冲击导致任务失败的规则保持原版。

A speeding ticket may still cause a separate traffic-offence penalty.
超速罚款仍可能触发独立的交通违章扣分。

Other satisfaction rewards retain their original values.
其他满意度奖励保持原值。

Requires the Coaches DLC and targets ETS2 1.62.x, based on version 1.62.3.2.
需要 Coaches DLC，面向 ETS2 1.62.x，基于 1.62.3.2 制作。

On first installation or when switching from versioned filenames, disable older packages and activate happy_passengers.scs once in the current profile's Mod Manager.
首次安装或从带版本号的文件名切换时，停用旧版安装包，并在当前存档的 MOD 管理器中手动启用一次 happy_passengers.scs。

For later compatible updates, exit the game and overwrite the same happy_passengers.scs file without renaming it.
后续更新兼容版本时，先退出游戏，再覆盖同名的 happy_passengers.scs 文件，不要重命名。

Give this mod higher priority than other mods changing the same coach economy or chassis definitions.
将本 MOD 的优先级设为高于其他修改相同客车经济或底盘配置的 MOD。

Truck chassis, global physics definitions and user stability settings are not modified.
不修改货车底盘、全局物理配置或用户稳定性设置。

Static configuration and package integrity checks have passed, and the user has reported working zero penalties.
静态配置与安装包完整性检查已通过，用户已反馈零扣分生效。

The 10 km reward interval and handling improvements have not yet been verified in game.
10 km 奖励间隔及操控改善幅度尚未游戏内验证。
